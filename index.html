page iii · a letter from the studio

“Why does everything in India have a story — except what’s on your feet?”

That question started all of this. We grew up in a country where every street, every dish, every festival carries a narrative — and then we looked down. Socks have always been meaningless. So we hope moje give you a way to be free, to express.

We knit in small chapters and we never restock — not to be precious, but because a story you can reprint forever isn’t a story, it’s a pattern. Every design is rooted in a place, a feeling, a moment1. Every pair is 320 GSM cotton, made fully in India2. And every chapter closes3, so the people who caught it become part of it.

Sometimes to win, you gotta stay alive. We’re still here. Still writing.

— naval, founder
