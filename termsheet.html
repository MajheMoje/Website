01What this is A dedicated section on majhemoje.in called Everyday Moje (its own menu item and page), opening with a Corbel × Majhe Moje creative. Majhe Moje curates selected Corbel SKUs into bestsellers, six exclusive story bundles and the full everyday catalogue, writes the stories and runs the storefront. Corbel supplies, packs and ships.
02Money
  • Customer pays Majhe Moje. Of each net sale (after discounts, excluding GST and shipping charged), 60% to Corbel, covering product, packing and forward shipping, and 40% to Majhe Moje.
  • Settlement: Majhe Moje pays Corbel’s share every 15 days for delivered orders, net of refunds.
  • To agree: who bears payment-gateway fees (proposed: shared 60/40).
03Pricing & parity
  • Singles sell at Corbel’s own listed price and compare-at price on mycorbel.com.
  • Story bundles are priced by Majhe Moje, at or below the sum of Corbel prices inside.
  • Corbel gives 7 days’ notice of any sale or price change on listed SKUs, so both stores stay in sync. our ask
04Fulfilment
  • Orders passed to Corbel the same day (shared order sheet or Shopify app, format to agree).
  • Dispatch within 48 hours; tracking shared with Majhe Moje within 24 hours of dispatch. our ask
  • Packed in Corbel’s packaging with the Majhe Moje insert card in every order (supplied by Majhe Moje). An optional Majhe Moje outer cover can follow later.
  • Corbel shares stock levels for listed SKUs daily and flags any stock-out before it happens.
05Returns & COD
  • Corbel covers defects, damage in transit and wrong items: replacement or refund, including reverse shipping.
  • Majhe Moje covers change-of-mind returns and failed or refused COD deliveries (the courier cost of the return).
  • Returned goods in resaleable condition go back into Corbel’s stock.
06Customers Majhe Moje owns the customer relationship. Corbel receives only what’s needed to ship, uses it only to ship, adds no Corbel marketing or flyers to parcels, and doesn’t contact these customers.
07Brand & display
  • Majhe Moje displays selected Corbel SKUs, credited as “knitted by Corbel”, with a link to mycorbel.com.
  • Corbel doesn’t display, sell or use Majhe Moje’s own products, Chapters, designs or imagery. A text-only “stocked at Majhe Moje” mention is fine, with Majhe Moje’s approval.
  • Bundle names, stories, the insert card and the page are Majhe Moje’s. Corbel won’t recreate these bundles or names on its own channels. our ask
08Photography
  • Corbel permits Majhe Moje to use its product photos and to restyle them, including with AI tools, for this section only.
  • Restyles never change the product itself (colour, pattern, knit, logo). Corbel can flag any image it thinks misrepresents a product.
  • Corbel provides one free sample of each listed SKU for colour-matching and a Majhe Moje shoot. our ask
09Term An initial 6 months, with a review at 90 days on sales, dispatch times, returns and ratings. Either side can end it with 30 days’ notice; open orders are fulfilled either way. The SKU list can change by mutual agreement at any time.
10Next steps
  • Agree these terms in principle, and confirm the 66-product catalogue, bestsellers and size ranges.
  • Corbel sends samples and photo permission; Majhe Moje restyles and builds the section.
  • Test the order flow end to end with 5 test orders, then go live.
