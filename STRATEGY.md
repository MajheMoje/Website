# Rojnishi × Corbel — the everyday line

*A strategy for making majhe moje everyone's socks, without spending the
premium that Chapters built.*

---

## 1. The insight

**Majhe moje means "my socks."** A name like that can't belong only to
collectors. Right now the brand has one way in: Chapters, which are designer
first editions at ₹499+ a pair, printed once and never restocked. That's the
right product for someone who wants to *collect* a story. It doesn't work for
the commuter on the 5:47 local who needs seven pairs a week and wears them
until they're done.

If the name means *my* socks, the brand needs a second shelf that covers
everyone's working week.

## 2. Brand architecture: two books, one shelf

Keep the publishing metaphor the new site is built on and extend it:

|                 | **Chapters**                    | **Rojnishi** (रोजनिशी)                        |
|-----------------|---------------------------------|-----------------------------------------------|
| Metaphor        | First editions                  | The everyday diary                            |
| Promise         | *One printing, ever*            | *Always in print*                             |
| Stories         | Written & drawn by the studio   | Taken from working days, including customers' |
| Made            | Small runs                      | Knitted with Corbel, at scale                 |
| Job to be done  | Collect, gift, keep             | Wear, wash, wear out                          |
| Price (proposed)| from ₹499 · box ₹1,999          | ₹349 a pair · ₹899 for three · The Week ₹1,899 |
| Visual code     | Cream paper, book typography    | Ruled school notebook, red margin, kraft cover |

**Why "Rojnishi":** it's the Marathi word for a diary (रोज, *roj* = every
day). It sits naturally next to "majhe moje," says *everyday* without saying
*basic*, and gives the line its own story device: every design is an **entry**
about a working day, stamped with a time.

**Why it doesn't dilute Chapters.** The value of "one printing, ever" rests on
Chapters staying scarce. Rojnishi protects that in four ways:

1. **Different artwork.** Rojnishi never reuses a Chapter design, so a Chapter
   pair can't be bought as an everyday reprint.
2. **Different visual code.** Chapters look like a book; Rojnishi looks like a
   ruled notebook. Customers can tell at a glance which shelf they're on.
3. **A clear price step.** ₹349 (Rojnishi) against ₹499+ (Chapters), and
   Rojnishi is never discounted into Chapter territory.
4. **A route upward.** An entry that resonates can be "bound" into a future
   Chapter as a designer edition. Rojnishi can feed Chapters, never the
   other way round.

## 3. The line: a day in the diary

Seven launch entries follow one working day in Mumbai and end on a Sunday.
Each is matched to a sock base Corbel **already manufactures**, so development
is mostly artwork, not new tooling.

| Time  | Entry            | For                                              | Corbel base it maps to                 |
|-------|------------------|--------------------------------------------------|----------------------------------------|
| 05:47 | The First Local  | Commuters, early shifts                          | Bamboo crew (odour-resistant)          |
| 09:00 | Shutter Up       | Shopkeepers, chemists, cashiers: standing all day | Cushioned ankle / sports ankle         |
| 13:00 | The Long Walk    | Field reps, postmen, site supervisors            | Bamboo sports crew (cushioned)         |
| 16:00 | Cutting Chai     | Desks, classrooms, the 4 o'clock break           | Bamboo formal crew                     |
| 19:30 | The Last Mile    | Delivery riders, bikers, monsoon boots           | Bamboo biker socks (mid-calf)          |
| 23:00 | Night Shift      | Nurses, guards, bakers, call-centre floors       | Cushioned bamboo crew                  |
| Sun   | The Sunday Match | Gully cricket, the 7 a.m. turf                   | Football Aero-Knit / sports crew       |

**The Week**, a seven-pair box with one entry per day, is the hero SKU. People
already buy everyday socks in multipacks, and a seven-day box turns that habit
into a story. It's packaged like a brown-paper-covered school notebook with
the classic name label ("Name: yours. Subject: the week.").

Sizes follow the brand's existing split: UK 4–7 and UK 7–10.

## 4. The Corbel collaboration model

**Roles.** Majhe moje handles stories, design, brand, community and
storefront. Corbel handles knitting, fibres, base-sock engineering and
fulfilment capacity.

**Recommended structure: manufacture now, co-brand openly.**

- **Phase 1: contract manufacture on existing bases.** Corbel knits
  majhe-moje-designed socks on sock bases it already produces. This is the
  fastest, lowest-risk way to learn MOQs, lead times and landed cost.
- **Credit the maker, publisher-style.** Every book names its printer in the
  colophon. Rojnishi packaging and the website credit Corbel as *the press*:
  "knitted with Corbel." That's honest, and it gives Corbel brand value, which
  is a reason for them to offer better terms.
- **Phase 2: co-listing.** Once sell-through is proven, list "majhe moje ×
  Corbel" Rojnishi on Corbel's own store as well. Corbel gets story-led
  product; majhe moje gets Corbel's everyday-sock audience.

**Terms to settle with Corbel before launch:**

- MOQ per design × size × base, and whether seven designs can share a run
- Landed cost per pair at 500 / 2,000 / 5,000 pairs (this sets whether ₹349 holds)
- Artwork exclusivity: Rojnishi designs are majhe moje IP, not for Corbel's
  own range
- Quality spec sign-off per base (fibre blend, cushioning zones, cuff rib, wash test)
- Replenishment lead time: "always in print" is a promise, so stock-outs
  have to be rare
- Co-listing commercials (margin split or wholesale price) for Phase 2
- Bulk and B2B pricing tiers (see §6)

## 5. Stories of the common man: where they come from

"Culture, not costume" is a house value, so Rojnishi must never caricature
working people. The stories should come *from* them.

- **Send us your day.** The Rojnishi page invites anyone to describe what
  their working day asks of their feet. Each season the studio picks entries
  to turn into designs.
- **Credit the storyteller.** If an entry is chosen, the person's first name,
  city and work go on the story card packed with every pair, and the first
  box is theirs. "Majhe moje" then literally becomes someone's socks.
- **The launch seven are archetypes, not invented people.** No made-up
  testimonials. Named credits begin with the first round of real submissions.

This is also the community engine. Every submission is a customer telling
you, unprompted, what your next product should be.

## 6. Kit the whole shift (B2B)

This is the biggest volume opportunity for "everyone's socks." The Studio
already sells custom branded socks, so extend it:

- **Workforce kits** for hospitals (Night Shift), delivery fleets (The Last
  Mile), retail chains (Shutter Up), schools and offices (Cutting Chai).
- **Commissioned entries**: an employer commissions an entry about their own
  people's work, which puts the Studio's storytelling into a uniform.
- Corbel's bulk-order capacity makes this feasible. Majhe moje sells the
  story; Corbel fulfils the volume.

## 7. Phasing

| Phase | What | Proves |
|-------|------|--------|
| **0 · Terms** | MOQ, landed cost and exclusivity agreed with Corbel; wear-test samples on 3 bases | Unit economics |
| **1 · Pilot** | 3 entries (The First Local, Night Shift, The Last Mile) + "send us your day" live on majhemoje.in | Demand, repeat rate |
| **2 · The Week** | All 7 entries + The Week box; first storyteller credits; co-listing on Corbel | Hero SKU, partnership |
| **3 · Kit the shift** | B2B workforce kits, first commissioned entry | Volume channel |
| **4 · Beyond** | Marketplaces, selective offline retail; best entries bound into a Chapter | Reach, halo loop |

## 8. What to measure

- **Repeat purchase rate at 60/90 days.** Everyday socks live or die on
  replenishment.
- **Pairs per order** and **Week-box attach rate.**
- **Story submissions per month**, a proxy for community.
- **Cross-shelf movement.** What share of Rojnishi buyers later buy a Chapter?
  That shows whether the halo loop works.
- **Chapter sell-through and price integrity.** This is the guardrail: if
  Chapters start needing discounts, the lines are too close.

## 9. Guardrails

- Never restock a Chapter, and never reprint Chapter artwork in Rojnishi.
- Never discount Rojnishi below its "always in print" price floor. Everyday
  doesn't have to mean on sale.
- Stories come from people, not stereotypes. Credit them.
- Always credit Corbel as the maker. Don't hide the press.
