how it works

chosen by us. knitted by corbel.

  1. we pick

    We choose the pairs from Corbel that earn a place in a story. Most don’t make the cut.

  2. corbel knits & ships

    Straight from Corbel, packed with our story card, usually out in 48 hours.

  3. you scan

    The card’s QR shows your story, how to size and care for your pairs, and a code for next time.

  4. you tell us

    Rate your pair. Your reviews decide which stories stay on the shelf.
